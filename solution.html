The Solution

From raw signal to decided action.

PR×ESG watches every public ESG signal, reconciles it against a company's own claims and its peers, scores the risk, and drafts the response — continuously.

How it works

Five steps. One continuous loop.

01

Ingest

Every public ESG signal — filings, news, social, NGO, satellite, customs, litigation.

02

Structure

AI converts documents into atomic, source-traced, framework-mapped claims.

03

Graph

Claims linked into a temporal knowledge graph; contradictions surface automatically.

04

Reason

Risk, sentiment and predictive models score, forecast and draft — with evidence.

05

Act

Briefs, responses and crisis playbooks pre-drafted for one-click human approval.

Worked example

A company's “12% emissions cut” claim is ingested from its filing, cross-checked against its earnings call, a journalist's story and satellite methane data — scored, flagged for the one contradiction that matters, and answered with a pre-drafted response. Elapsed: minutes.
